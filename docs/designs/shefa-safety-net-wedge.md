## Problem Statement

Bangladeshi diagnostic centers and clinics run a front-desk that is
structurally unable to answer the phone during fixed daily windows. A single
operator simultaneously manages walk-in crowds, cash/card billing, and the
multi-line phone set. During lunch hours, prayer times, and shift handovers
the desk is physically unattended — the phone rings, no one answers, and the
caller either retries or books with the next clinic. Large networks (Ibn Sina
operates ~30 Dhaka branches) compound this with a number maze: every branch
keeps its own landline and mobile numbers, so patients burn time finding the
right line only to hit an unanswered one.

This design narrows the 2026-09-22 strategy ("SHEFA First-Clinic Strategy —
Shadow Line + Door-Opener") from two parallel wedges to one door-shaped wedge:
**SHEFA answers the specific unmanned windows, captures the caller as a lead,
and hands the resolution to the desk.** Confirmed bookings, network telephony,
HMS/SIP integration, and push-selling follow-up calls all stay in the roadmap —
they are the land-and-expand phase two, deliberately absent from the wedge and
from the pitch.

The builder is still building from OUTSIDE the market: fully-local stack
(Python + qwen2.5:7b + Whisper STT + Bangla VITS/Piper TTS + open-source local
telephony — Asterisk/FreeSWITCH — with a Bangladeshi SIP trunk), no named
clinic owner, no users, no measured demand evidence, and — critically — the
full SIP-trunk→Asterisk/FreeSWITCH→pipeline voice loop has NEVER been run
end-to-end with a real call.

## Demand Evidence

Zero since 2026-09-22. No clinic reached, nothing measured, no named person in
reach. The conviction-sale baseline is unchanged: the pitch lives or dies on
whether the pain can be proven inside a real org. The global category is
validated (MedCalls, Sully.ai, Clara/VoxAI/AI VOX safety-net tiers at
$150-500/mo) — which proves the problem is real and priced, but does NOT prove
a single Dhaka owner will say yes.

Demand evidence must now be MANUFACTURED by the mechanism itself: the one-week
live trial of Safety-Net on a real clinic line produces real-caller behavior —
leads captured, callbacks answered, transcripts — which IS the evidence.

## Status Quo

Known in detail from lived market knowledge (not from inside a clinic):

- One overloaded desk employee handles walk-ins, multi-doctor appointment
  books, and cash simultaneously; phone answering is the interruptible task.
- Fixed daily windows (lunch, prayer, shift handover) leave lines fully
  unmanned; calls drop, revenue is permanently lost.
- Multi-branch networks: each branch has its own landline/mobile set; patients
  face friction finding the right number and sitting in manual queues.
- Estimated "renowned clinic" volume: 2,000+ appointment calls per month across
  departments (UNMEASURED — belief, not data).
- No call-detail records exist at the target clinic; the number one buyer
  truth — "how many calls do you actually miss?" — cannot currently be answered.

## Target User & Narrowest Wedge

- Single-branch independent clinics (Dhanmondi, Uttara, Mirpur, Gulshan area).
- One branch line of a multi-branch network (e.g. Ibn Sina) — the network play

Buyer chain: clinic owner/counter (decision-maker), front-desk operator

Narrowest wedge: **"your line drops calls during the same windows every day.
Let SHEFA answer those windows for one week and show you what it catches."**
The value unit is the CALLBACK LEAD — name + number + reason captured in Bangla,
texted to the desk, desk resolves. No booking trust required on day one.

## Constraints

- Pre-product: no named clinic, no users, no demand evidence, conviction sale.
- Loop unproven: the full Twilio→MediaStream→STT→LLM→TTS→TwiML voice path has
  never been exercised end to end with a real call (premise P2).
- Local hardware: RTX 4060 Ti 16GB, 1-2 simultaneous calls max during pilot —
  adequate for the wedge, inadequate for the 2,000+/mo claim (deferred).
- Bangla-first: the loop must prove the Bangla voice capture, not just English.
- On-prem positioning: data stays on-site is the END-STATE story; week-one data
  physically lives on the builder's machine (state explicitly in consent).
- Complementary to incumbent HMS: never a replacement day one; no HMS
  integration in the wedge (deferred to phase two).
- Health telecom data governed: disclosure, retention T+14, read-only access.
- Building from outside the market; no org in reach yet.

## Premises

1. Wedge = Safety-Net Capture: SHEFA answers the named unmanned windows, greets
   in Bangla, captures name + callback number + reason in natural conversation,
   texts the desk every lead, and transfers emergencies. It is a door-opener,
   NOT a network play. Network telephony / HMS / SIP / push-selling stays in the
   roadmap, never in the pitch.
2. The answering loop DOES NOT WORK end-to-end yet. The SIP-trunk→Asterisk/
   FreeSWITCH→pipeline loop must be proven (and latency-measured per turn)
   before any outreach. First dogfood call: a simulated real call on the local
   stack.
3. ONE pitch object goes to both the single-branch list and the Ibn Sina-type
   list — never two builds. The front-desk drop-call story lands identically
   on both.
4. REVISED (in-session, after cross-model challenge): demand evidence is zero
   and unmeasured; a demo proves technical feasibility ONLY. Evidence is
   manufactured by a ONE-WEEK LIVE trial of Safety-Net on the clinic's own
   line — real caller behavior (leads captured, callbacks answered, volumes)
   is the evidence. This folds the 9/22 shadow-line into the answering wedge.

## Approaches Considered

### Approach A: Safety-Net Capture (minimal viable — CHOSEN)
Shallow answering agent. SHEFA covers the named unmanned windows in Bangla,
captures name + callback + reason in natural conversation, texts the desk every
lead, transfers emergencies. No booking trust required. Desk resolves.
- Effort: S–M (loops exist in pieces; wires together with a constrained prompt
  and lead-log).
- Risk: Low–Med.
- Pros: smallest possible yes this week; validated global tier (VoxAI €149
  "Safety Net", Clara $299 Essentials, AI VOX audit-first); needs only one
  branch line's permission to cover 60-90 min/day; produces live-caller
  evidence per P4.
- Cons: undersells SHEFA's full dialogue; desk must still resolve the callback
  (value is a captured lead, not a booking, week one); Bangla production
  quality at phone-line audio is unproven.
- Reuses: /chat + session manager + system prompt (configs/prompts/system.md)
  + test-call page; Twilio-specific /incoming + /media-stream endpoints get
  replaced by the Asterisk/FreeSWITCH media bridge (existing STT/LLM/TTS pipe
  carries over unchanged).

### Approach B: Live-Trial Coverage Agent (ideal architecture)
Full first-line answering agent: complete Twilio→WSS→STT→LLM→TTS→TwiML loop,
appointment booking, complete "today's calls" log. The entire 41-item plan
shrinks to this, proven live on one clinic's line for 7 days.
- Effort: L; Risk: Med–High.
- Pros: the real product; produces the demo theater of a machine completing a
  Bangla booking; richest evidence (booking intents + latencies).
- Cons: heaviest build before the first yes; booking trust is the LAST thing an
  owner grants a stranger, not the first; violates the wedge discipline the
  session just established.
- Reuses: full pipeline + Asterisk/FreeSWITCH + session manager + redis caching
  + tests.

### Approach C: Demo Theater + Shadow Line (lateral — 9/22 original)
Recorded 60s Bangla demo + one-pager + passive shadow line in parallel, walk
into 10-20 clinics with THEIR own missed-call numbers, then pitch the agent.
- Effort: S (for the shadow line); Risk: Low.
- Pros: manufactures the volume evidence (premises #2 measurement precondition
  from 9/22); zero product-trust needed — it's read-only.
- Cons: user rejected the shadow line as the wedge on 9/22 and again today; a
  passive log alone cannot cap the evidence gap in the same week (defines WHAT
  is missed, not WHAT SHEFA catches); retains two separate sales stories.

**RECOMMENDATION: Approach A.** It is the smallest thing a clinic owner lets a
stranger touch their phone line for — cover the known gap, capture the lead,
hand it back. It produces live-caller evidence (P4) in the same week it runs,
and it is the exact reference-model the global market already prices at
$150-500/mo, so the story is credible when pitched. B is the upgrade path after
the owner's yes; C was rejected in-session and on 9/22.

## Safety-Net Agent Behavior (wedge scope)

- Coverage: configurable named windows (e.g. 1:30-2:30 PM lunch, 4:30-5:00
  PM prayer-adjacent). Phones ring to SHEFA only in the window; desk takes
  over outside it (forward-on-no-answer style, N rings LOW for the wedge).
- Telephony path (rev 1): a Bangladeshi SIP trunk terminates the clinic's
  incoming calls into a local Asterisk/FreeSWITCH; the B2BUA bridges the PSTN
  leg to the ASR/TTS pipeline. Forward-on-no-answer during the window is a
  trunk/carrier feature — screen for it explicitly. No cloud telephony
  provider in the media path; the SIP operator supplies PSTN reachability only.
- Greeting: Bangla-first, disclosure included ("this line is answered by an
  automated assistant today; calls may be recorded to improve service").
- Capture loop: caller name → callback number (read-back confirmation) →
  reason (structured intents: appointment / result inquiry / billing / other).
- Handoff: callback lead texted to the designated desk number + logged locally.
- Escalation: emergency keywords → immediate transfer to a human (conference /
  warm handoff); no medical advice, never.
- Degrade path: on STT/TTS/LLM failure → fixed courtesy message + capture
  callback number as DTMF / fall back to transfer.
- Metrics logged per call: transcript, intent, outcome, transfer status,
  audio gap + per-turn latency (must meet the ≤2s target from the plan).

## Open Questions

- Can the target clinic's number forward on no-answer to the SHEFA line during
  the window? Screening ask #1 (as 9/22). If not forwardable, the wedge
  degrades to "SHEFA holds its own published number in the window" — weaker.
- Which Bangladeshi SIP trunk / IP-phone operator supplies inbound DID +
  forward-on-no-answer at acceptable per-minute cost? Confirm the trunk path
  before promising week-one (hard spend cap ~$10 for the trial week,
  owner-approved above it); Asterisk/FreeSWITCH run fully local once the trunk
  lands.
- Bangla quality at phone-line audio: Whisper large-v3 + Bangla VITS latency
  and accuracy during the constrained capture loop on the local GPU.
- Which first 5-10 named clinics: warm referral path vs cold list? (SCOPE from
  9/22's 10-20: fewer targets, same-week loop proof first.)
- What does the desk do with a texted lead today — is WhatsApp to a desk line
  the right hand-off channel?

## Data Handling & Privacy (health telecom data)

- Caller disclosure on line: state clearly that the line is answered by an
  automated assistant and may be recorded. No silent transcription.
- Retention: transcripts held only for the trial window, then deleted T+14 days
  after the trial week unless the owner opts into keeping them.
- Access during trial: owner sees "today's calls" via read-only view over
  private tunnel; shown to owner + builder only; nothing leaves the box.
- Week-one hardware is PINNED to the BUILDER's machine (no clinic box exists);
  the one-pager consent states this explicitly — data-stays-on-site is the END
  state, not week-one reality.
- Verify Bangladesh telecom consent law before the first forwarded call.

## Success Criteria

- Loop proven on local stack: one simulated real call completes Bangla greeting
  → lead capture → read-back; per-turn latency ≤2s; degrade path exercised.
- One named clinic owner grants live-line access to try Safety-Net for one week
  (either bucketed list) within two weeks of outreach.
- One week of real-caller data collected: lead count, callback volume, call
  volume across the covered windows — testing the 2,000+/mo belief for the
  FIRST time against reality.
- If measured volume is far below the story, pivot to staff-time / abandoned-
  call math (measurement precondition) — do NOT stretch original claims.
- Owner sees a completed Safety-Net lead capture and says yes to the FOLLOW-UP:
  a paid pilot of the full answering agent.

## Go / No-Go Gate (revised for the one-wedge posture)

After 2 weeks: loop proven + outreach to 5-10 clinics:
- GO: >=1 owner granted a one-week Safety-Net live trial (or committed to a
  pilot demo).
- NARROW: 0 qualified yeses but warm referrals exist — re-run outreach through
  referrals for one more week.
- STOP/CLOSE: 0 yeses, 0 warm referrals — suspend the wedge; reframe the pitch
  object (e.g. patient-experience story) and only then retry fresh. If the loop
  cannot be closed within 2 weeks, STOP and re-scope — the product premise is
  not yet buildable.
