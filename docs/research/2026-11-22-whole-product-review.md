# SHEFA whole-product review: 7 perspectives (2026-11-22)

Generated by /plan-ceo-review (scope expansion, strategy depth), branch `feat/dialogue-manager`.
Question asked: while the voice data is being collected, what must be rock-solid in everything
else so that a better speech model turns straight into market advantage and revenue?

Evidence base: the code as of commit `7d90441` (2,094 tests passing); live calls driven through the
public link on 2026-11-21; `docs/designs/shefa-conversation-first-platform.md` (the chosen
direction); `docs/deployment.md` ("What is NOT verified"); a web scan. Nothing here is claimed
about real clinics or real callers: there are still none.

---

## Why these 7 perspectives

A voice receptionist only succeeds if **the caller** gets booked, **the desk** trusts it,
**the buyer** sees money and pays, nothing **unsafe** ever happens, it **never goes silent**, the
**business** can grow on no budget, and it **scales** past one clinic without the founder
hand-holding each one. Each of the 7 is a way SHEFA can fail even with a perfect speech model.

| # | Perspective | The question it answers |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | The caller (patient on a phone) | Does a real Bangladeshi caller end the call booked? |
| 2 | The front desk | Will the receptionist work *with* SHEFA, or quietly switch it off? |
| 3 | The buyer (owner / hospital executive) | Can they see it earns more than it costs, and will they pay? |
| 4 | Safety and trust | Can one bad call end the company? |
| 5 | Reliability and operations | What happens at 2 am when something breaks and nobody is watching? |
| 6 | Market and business model | Who pays, how much, why us, and how does it grow with no budget? |
| 7 | Scale and onboarding | Can clinic #2 to #100 go live without the founder doing it by hand? |

**Left out on purpose:**
- *Doctor:* their needs reach SHEFA through the desk and the buyer.
- *Investor:* there's no fundraising now; a paying buyer is the only proof that counts.
- *Code quality:* 2,094 tests pass, so it isn't the bottleneck.
- *Model research:* the voice collection already covers it, and you said the model comes later.
- *UI design:* folded into perspectives 2 and 3.

## 1. The caller

**Solid**
- Booking is led in code with one short question at a time, not left to the language model:
  who, day, time, name, number, read-back, yes. 225 call variants pass (`tests/unit/test_guided_matrix.py`).
- Off-script questions get honest answers from the doctor list or from the clinic's own facts,
  never made up:
  - facilities: parking, ambulance, hours…
  - "who is the best doctor", "is there a woman doctor", "who are you";
  - looking up an existing booking by phone.
- Live calls on 2026-11-21, through the public link with the real model, answered in 1 to 4 seconds.
  They correctly handled:
  - a department said the mixed way people say it ("নিউরোলজি বিভাগ");
  - "কাল সকালে হবে?" given when SHEFA asked which doctor;
  - a name given early;
  - an emergency (999 and hand-over).

**Needs improving**
- **Bookings are time slots only, but Bangladeshi chambers mostly work by serial number.** Callers
  literally say "সিরিয়াল দিতে চাই". The clinic store has `slot_minutes` and no serial concept. If the
  target hospital books "serial 14, chamber 5 pm to 9 pm", SHEFA's "10:30, 10:45 or 11:00?" is the
  wrong product. This open question from 9 Nov is still unanswered, and **it's the biggest product risk.**
- **Phone numbers by voice fail.** Every end-to-end test lost digits ("0111223344"). On a real
  phone line the caller ID gives the number, but booking for a relative and the web channel still
  need it said aloud.
- Spoken grammar: "ডাক্তার নাসরিন-এর" (hyphenated possessive), and an abrupt "ডাক্তার নাসরিন।"
  before the times.
- Never measured: Bangla recognition on 8 kHz phone audio, and whether the Bangla voice sounds
  natural to Bangladeshi listeners.

**Feature requests**
1. **Serial-number booking mode** (per doctor: serial or slot): "আপনার সিরিয়াল ১৪, চেম্বার
   বিকেল ৫টায় শুরু, আনুমানিক ৭টার দিকে আসবেন।"
2. **Type the number on the keypad** (DTMF) when speech fails twice: "নম্বরটা ফোনের বোতাম চেপে দিন।"
3. **Repeat callers:** "আগের বার ডাক্তার কবিরকে দেখিয়েছিলেন, এবারও ওনার কাছে?" The lookup
   code already finds past bookings.
4. **Booking for someone else** ("আমার বাবার জন্য"): a patient name separate from the caller,
   on the same phone.

## 2. The front desk

**Solid**
- Phone bookings land unconfirmed until the desk taps confirm.
- Unclear calls become a call-back with a reason (Live page, refreshes every 5 s).
- Staff accounts and roles; appointments and a call log with transcripts.

**Needs improving**
- A hand-over or call-back only appears in a list the desk must be looking at. No sound, no
  phone alert. In a busy reception that means it gets missed.
- Paper is the desk's real system. There's no printable "today's serials per doctor" sheet, so
  the desk keeps two systems.
- Walk-ins: if the desk can't add a walk-in in two taps, SHEFA's free times become wrong and it
  double-books.

**Feature requests**
1. A **sound and pop-up when SHEFA needs a person**, with a one-line summary
   ("রহিম, নিউরোলজি, কাল সকালে চান, নম্বর বোঝা যায়নি").
2. **Print today's list** per doctor (A4, Bangla).
3. **Add a walk-in** in two taps; it takes the next serial or slot.
4. **Warm transfer:** the desk phone rings and hears the summary before the caller is put through.

## 3. The buyer (owner / hospital executive)

**Solid**
- An Overview page, call log, appointments, audit trail and data export.
- Templates already exist for a pilot proposal, data-processing agreement and support terms
  (`docs/business/`).

**Needs improving**
- **No proof-of-value numbers.** Nothing shows: calls answered while the desk was busy or
  closed, bookings made by SHEFA, missed calls recovered, hand-over rate, or staff time saved.
  The buyer pays for that report, not for the AI.
- **The SMS confirmation is fake.** `integrations/notifications.py` builds the message, throws it
  away, and returns `"sent": True`. A review flagged this on 2026-10-01 and it's still not fixed.
  A clinic told "patients get an SMS" would find out from an angry patient.
- Missed-call call-back was step 3 of the chosen build order and isn't built. Outbound calling
  exists (`telephony/outbound.py`, reminders), so the parts are there.

**Feature requests**
1. A **weekly "what SHEFA did for you" report** in Bangla, one page that can be sent on WhatsApp:
   calls taken, bookings, after-hours bookings, missed calls recovered, hand-overs, no-shows.
2. **Real confirmations and reminders**, by SMS through a Bangladeshi SMS gateway (or WhatsApp
   later), with delivery status. Remove the fake success.
3. **Missed-call call-back:** a call the desk didn't pick up is called back by SHEFA within N
   minutes, using the same guided flow.
4. **Shadow-mode pilot report:** SHEFA listens alongside the desk for two weeks and the report
   shows what it would have booked. This is how a cautious hospital says yes.

## 4. Safety and trust

**Solid**
- Emergency phrases, including a detector for symptoms said together ("বুকে ব্যথা" near "শ্বাস"),
  have recall tests. "Call 999" plus hand-over was proven on a live call.
- The greeting says SHEFA is an AI. A filter blocks medical advice. Facility and directory
  answers are never invented.
- Transcripts are encrypted at rest. Access to patient data is audited. The admin side is never
  reachable from the public link.
- Booking lookup gives the same reply whether or not a number exists, and limits tries.

**Needs improving**
- The fake SMS success (above) is also a trust failure.
- The booking-lookup lock lives in memory, so a restart resets it. There's no SMS one-time code (OTP).
- Recording product calls for training needs a consent line and a legal basis for the real
  product, separate from the volunteers' consent.
- Never measured: does emergency detection still work on noisy 8 kHz audio?
- Nobody is named as owner of the emergency script (the "demo safety sign-off" in TODOS.md).

**Feature requests**
1. A **safety log**: every emergency, hand-over and refused medical question in one list, reviewed weekly.
2. A **monthly red-team set of calls** (noise, slang, a child calling, a drunk caller, a
   threatening caller) run against every release.
3. A one-page **incident runbook** with a named person.

## 5. Reliability and operations

**Solid**
- vLLM measured at p95 1.56 s for 5 callers.
- A capacity limit with a busy message and call-back. Asterisk falls back to dialing the desk when
  SHEFA is down.
- Services restart themselves after a crash. There are health checks, backups, a pause switch and
  a public-link switch.

**Needs improving**
- **No real call has ever gone through a real phone trunk.** This is the single biggest unknown
  in the whole product, ahead of the speech model.
- Speech recognition is the bottleneck: about 3.3 s worst-case turn at 3 callers, against a 2.5 s target.
- **No monitoring or alerts.** If SHEFA dies at 2 am, the founder finds out from the clinic.
  There are no metrics (no Prometheus or similar); only logs.
- One machine with one GPU is a single point of failure. The Asterisk fallback must be tested,
  not assumed.

**Feature requests**
1. A **heartbeat with a phone alert** (Telegram bot or SMS) to the founder when calls fail,
   latency jumps or the box goes quiet.
2. A **nightly test call** that books, checks and cancels, and alerts on failure.
3. **Latency per turn** (speech, model, voice) recorded per call and shown to the owner as
   "average wait".
4. **Free 8 kHz test today:** a softphone (for example Zoiper) on the same network, connected to
   Asterisk with the G.711 codec. This measures real phone-quality audio with no trunk and no cost.

## 6. Market and business model

**Solid**
- The positioning is real and hard to copy: Bangla first, data stays in Bangladesh, open models,
  and a clean hand-over.
- **The Voice Commons is the moat.**
  - It collects consented, reviewed Bangla phone speech: hospital, internet provider and shop calls.
  - It includes mixed-language department names and phone numbers said the Bangladeshi way.
  - Competitors (Edesy, Verbex) market Bangla voice but publish no Bangladeshi hospital numbers.
  - Nobody found has this data.
- There's a warm lead: the founder's uncle's hospital group.

**Needs improving**
- **Still zero demand evidence.** The 9 Nov assignment (meet the person who runs the hospital's
  booking line; get call volume, missed-call rate, top 10 reasons, serial vs slot) isn't
  recorded as done. Every big product decision below depends on it.
- The target is large chains: slow procurement, IT committees, a long time to first money.
- **Self-hosted, one GPU per clinic, kills the economics for anyone smaller than a hospital.**

**The bigger idea (first principles)**
- ePharma lists **38,000+ active doctor chambers** in Bangladesh where patients phone the
  chamber assistant for a serial ([ePharma](https://epharma.com.bd/en/doctors)). Its site says
  online booking is still "coming".
- Each chamber assistant misses calls during the rush. One SHEFA machine could serve many
  chambers:
  - the chamber forwards its busy or unanswered calls to a SHEFA number;
  - SHEFA gives the serial;
  - the doctor's assistant sees it on a phone.
- This needs the serial model (perspective 1), a hosted multi-tenant mode (perspective 7), and
  SMS. The data still stays in Bangladesh, on a machine you control, not a foreign cloud.
- Chains remain the big prize, but chambers can bring money and real call data in weeks instead
  of quarters.

**Feature requests**
1. **Ask before building:** the 6 questions to the uncle's booking-line person, plus 10 chamber
   assistants (15 minutes each, in person) about serial habits and missed calls.
2. **Price test:** a monthly fee per chamber or branch. Charge per booking only after you have numbers.
3. **Later:** the ISP and shop data already being collected makes SHEFA a general Bangla voice
   desk (internet support, e-commerce order status) once hospitals work. Don't build this now.

## 7. Scale and onboarding

**Solid**
- The clinic directory imports from one JSON file (`clinic/loader.py`).
- Config is one YAML file. `shefa doctor` checks the setup; there's a deployment guide, install
  scripts, start/stop icons and backups.

**Needs improving**
- **One install is one clinic.** There's no clinic or tenant id anywhere in the product data, so a
  hosted, many-clinic SHEFA is impossible today.
- Onboarding needs a hand-written JSON file of doctors, schedules and leave. No clinic will write
  it; the founder will, for every clinic.
- Updates are by hand on each machine, with no remote update and no version shown to the clinic.

**Feature requests**
1. **Clinic setup from a spreadsheet** (Excel/Google Sheet template in Bangla). Later, a photo of
   the chamber's schedule board, read by the model and checked by a person.
2. A **clinic id across the store** (a hosted, many-tenant mode), with each clinic's data separated
   and its own phone number.
3. A **version and update** command, with the version shown on the admin page.

## Failure modes registry (what can sink it)

| Failure | Caught today? | User sees | Fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| Patient told an SMS is coming, none arrives | No: code returns `sent: True` | Angry patient, clinic loses trust | D |
| Hospital books by serial, SHEFA offers clock times | No | Wrong product in the demo | A then C |
| SHEFA box dies at night | No alert | Calls ring out; clinic finds out first | F |
| Real trunk audio is worse than tests | Never measured | Booking fails on the first real call | B, then a trunk |
| Number misheard on a relative's booking | Asked again, then hand-over | Slow call, extra desk work | Keypad entry |
| Hand-over not noticed at the desk | Only a list | Caller never called back | Desk alert (2.1) |
| Emergency missed in noisy audio | Unmeasured | The worst case | Red-team set (4.2) |
| Founder sets up every clinic by hand | n/a | Growth stops at ~5 clinics | H, I |

## NOT in scope (now)

- Training a new speech model. The data collection continues, and training starts when there's
  enough reviewed data.
- Hospital information system integrations, until a buyer names one.
- WhatsApp chat booking; SMS comes first.
- The ISP and shop products, until hospitals work.

## What already exists and can be reused

- Outbound calls and reminders (`telephony/outbound.py`, `reminders.py`) for missed-call call-back.
- Booking lookup and past bookings (`pipeline/lookup.py`) for repeat callers.
- The call-back list and resolve API (`/api/v1/callbacks`) for desk alerts.
- The collector's backup, pause switch and public-link switch patterns for operations.
- The facilities table (`facilities:` in config) for each clinic's own facts.
